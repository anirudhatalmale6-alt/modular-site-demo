Photographs

Fifty years of the ridge — work parties, fetes, floods, frost and the long view from the top path.

Albums are added as they are scanned or taken. If you have photographs of the ridge — particularly anything before 1990 — the archive would be glad of them; bring the prints to the reading room and they will be scanned and returned to you the same week.
