Terms for the hire of the Old Reading Room

Reference
M-2025/09
Filed
30 September 2025
Issued by
The Committee

Rates from 1 January 2026

  • Residents, per hour: £6
  • Residents, all day (9am–6pm): £35
  • Non-residents, per hour: £11
  • Regular bookings, twelve or more sessions: 20 per cent discount
  • Children's parties: £30 flat, including the deposit below

Booking

Bookings are made with the secretary, in person or through the contact form, and are confirmed in writing. The hall diary on the noticeboard is the authoritative record; a booking is not held until it appears there.

Deposit and condition

A refundable deposit of £30 applies to all bookings other than committee business. It is returned within seven days provided the room is left as it was found, the heating is off and the key is returned.

Conditions of use

  1. The room holds sixty people seated and is licensed to nine o'clock.
  2. No alcohol may be sold without a licence, which is the hirer's responsibility to obtain.
  3. Fire exits must be kept clear. The hirer is responsible for knowing where they are.
  4. Nothing is to be fixed to the panelling. Free-standing display boards are in the store cupboard and may be used at no charge.

These terms replace those issued in March 2018, which are withdrawn.
