Minutes of the Annual General Meeting, 12 January 2026

Reference
M-2026/02
Filed
12 January 2026
Issued by
J. Amankwah, Secretary

Present

Ninety-one residents, representing seventy-eight households. The Chair took the meeting at 7.05pm in the Old Reading Room.

1. Minutes of the previous meeting

Accepted without amendment.

2. Treasurer's report

Presented in draft; the audited figures follow in memorandum M-2026/04. The reserve was reported as sound and the subscription held.

3. Elections

Nine members stood for re-election and were returned unopposed. Two vacancies were filled by M. Devlin and S. Oyelaran. The committee recorded its thanks to P. Innes, retiring after eleven years, whose work on the footpath adoption was described by the Chair as "the reason we have a footpath at all".

4. Motion: reading room opening hours

That the reading room open on Saturday mornings from April, staffed by volunteers on a rota.

Carried, 64 for, 11 against, 16 abstentions. A rota sheet is on the noticeboard.

5. Any other business

  • A resident asked whether the lighting scheme (M-2026/03) would affect the badger sett. The Works Sub-committee confirmed that two lamps would be omitted for that reason.
  • A resident asked that these minutes be published online within a week of each meeting rather than a month. The committee agreed.

The meeting closed at 8.40pm.
