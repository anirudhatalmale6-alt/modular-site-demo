Write to the committee, ask for a document from the archive, or report something that needs fixing on the common ground.

Where to find us

The committee office is at the back of the Old Reading Room, 14 Ridgeway Lane. Someone is usually there on Tuesday and Thursday mornings between 10am and 2pm. If the door is open, come in.

For anything that is not urgent, the form below is the fastest way to reach us — it goes straight to the secretary's inbox and is normally answered within two working days.

Reporting a problem

If you are reporting something practical — a light out, a fallen branch, a blocked gully — please say where it is as precisely as you can. "The third lamp past the postbox" is more useful than "the top of the road", and saves someone a walk.

Anything genuinely urgent — a fallen tree across the road, a burst main, a hazard to traffic — should go to the council's emergency line first and to us second.

Write to the committee

We use your address to reply and nothing else. It is not added to any list.

Other ways to reach us

  • Post: The Old Reading Room, 14 Ridgeway Lane, Harbour Ridge
  • Noticeboard: written notes left in the box outside the reading room are collected every Tuesday
  • In person: at any committee meeting, all of which are open to residents

We do not keep a mailing list and we do not pass on your details. Your email address is used to reply to you, and for nothing else.
