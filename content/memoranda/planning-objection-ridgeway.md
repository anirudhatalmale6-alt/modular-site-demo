## Position

The Association objects to the application as submitted. We take no position on whether the site should be built on at all; that is a matter for the local plan. Our objection is to this scheme, on three specific grounds.

## 1. Access

The proposed access sits eleven metres from the blind bend at the top of the lane. The applicant's transport statement counts traffic on a Tuesday in August, outside term time, and records forty-one movements in the peak hour. Our own count on a Wednesday in October recorded ninety-four. We ask that the count be repeated in term time before the application is determined.

## 2. Surface water

The site drains onto the lower footpath, which flooded twice in 2024 and once already this winter. The drainage strategy assumes an infiltration rate that the applicant's own borehole log does not support. We ask for a condition requiring attenuation on site.

## 3. The hedgerow

The eastern boundary hedge is shown for removal. It is marked on the 1842 tithe map and is, in our reading, protected under the Hedgerow Regulations 1997. If the officers disagree we would be glad to be shown why.

## What we are asking for

That the application be refused as submitted, or deferred until the traffic count is repeated and the drainage strategy revised. We would not object to a scheme that entered from the adopted road, retained the hedge and attenuated its own water.

A copy of this memorandum has been sent to the case officer and to the ward councillors.
