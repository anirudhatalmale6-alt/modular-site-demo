## Decision

The committee has accepted the quotation from Marlowe Electrical for the replacement of fourteen lanterns along the ridge path, at a cost of £6,740 including the removal of the old columns.

## Why now

The sodium lanterns were installed in 1998 with an expected life of twenty years. Six have failed in the last eighteen months and spares are no longer manufactured. Running costs for the replacement units are estimated at a fifth of the present figure.

## Colour and direction

Residents raised two concerns at the November meeting, and both have been carried into the specification:

1. The new units are warm-white at 2700K, not the blue-white commonly installed. They are dimmer than the lanterns they replace, not brighter.
2. All fittings are fully shielded and directed downward. There should be no light spilling into first-floor windows on the lane.

The two lamps closest to the badger sett will not be replaced. That stretch stays dark, as recommended by the county ecologist and as agreed at the meeting.

## Timing

Work begins in the second week of March and is expected to take eight working weeks. The path will remain open throughout, with short closures of no more than an hour while columns are lifted.

Residents with a specific difficulty — night working, mobility, a lamp directly outside a bedroom window — should tell the secretary before 1 March so it can be built into the programme rather than fixed afterwards.
