## Summary

Subscriptions were paid by 318 of 340 households, the highest figure since 2011. Income was £14,206 against expenditure of £11,940, leaving a working reserve of £9,318 at year end.

The largest single item of expenditure was the resurfacing of the lower footpath (£4,180), which came in £320 under the estimate given in memorandum M-2025/07.

## Notes on individual items

- **Grounds maintenance, £3,410.** Includes two additional cuts in the wet August.
- **Reading room, £2,090.** Heating, insurance and the annual electrical inspection.
- **Insurance, £1,260.** Public liability, renewed with the same insurer.
- **Events, £1,000.** Fully recovered from stall fees at the summer fete.

## Recommendation

That the subscription remain at £42 per household for 2026, and that £2,000 of the reserve be earmarked against the reading room roof, which the surveyor expects to need attention within five years.

The full ledger is available for inspection by any member at the committee office during opening hours.
