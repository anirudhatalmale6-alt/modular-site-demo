## The short version

Harbour Ridge Association was formed in 1974, when the ridge road was adopted and the residents who had built it were asked, for the first time, to look after it themselves. Fifty years later that is still the job: the verges, the footpaths, the reading room, the lighting, and the slow, patient business of speaking to the council on behalf of 340 households.

We are entirely voluntary. Nobody here is paid, and the subscription has not risen since 2019.

## What the committee does

- Maintains the common ground, the two footpaths and the reading room
- Publishes the minutes of every meeting, without exception, in the [memoranda archive](/memoranda)
- Represents residents on planning applications affecting the ridge
- Runs four events a year: the spring walk, the summer fete, the autumn work party and the carol evening
- Holds the photographic record of the ridge, going back to 1974

> We publish everything we can and we publish it quickly. A residents' association that keeps its own minutes private is not a residents' association — it is a private club with a mailing list.

## The committee

Chair, Secretary, Treasurer and eight elected members serve one-year terms. Elections are held at the annual general meeting each March, and any resident over sixteen may stand or vote.

If you would like to join the committee, or simply come to a meeting and see what it involves, [write to us](/contact). Meetings are open. Tea is provided and is not optional.

## How this site is run

The site is intentionally lightweight. Pages are plain files, photographs are folders of images, and the comments are held for moderation before they appear. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics script following you around.
