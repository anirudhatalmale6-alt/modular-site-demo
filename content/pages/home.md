## What is here

This site is deliberately small. It holds the things a residents' association actually needs to publish, and nothing else: who we are, how to reach us, the documents we have issued, the photographs we have taken, and a public comments section where anyone can respond.

Every page you see is one file. Adding another takes about a minute and does not require a developer.
